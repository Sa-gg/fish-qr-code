Place your Galunggong VALIDATION images here (separate from training images).

Suggested filenames:
  galunggong_val_001.jpg
  galunggong_val_002.jpg
  ...
  galunggong_val_005.jpg

Tips:
  - Use 3-5 images for validation when your dataset is very small.
  - These images must NOT be used in the training folder.
  - Validation images let the model check its accuracy on unseen data.

NOTE: Delete this folder if you are not using Galunggong.
